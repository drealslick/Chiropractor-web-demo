If a patient exhibits or reports symptoms indicative of a neurosurgical or medical emergency — including but not limited to:
* Sudden or progressive loss of bowel or bladder control (urinary retention or incontinence);
* Numbness, altered sensation, or paresthesia in the perineal, perianal, groin, or inner thigh ("saddle anesthesia") region;
* Rapidly progressing bilateral lower-extremity motor weakness or foot drop;
